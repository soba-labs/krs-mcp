# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in this project to be respectful, welcoming, and free from harassment. Be considerate in technical discussions, assume good intent, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `simon.budziak@sobalabs.ai`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is officially representing the project elsewhere.
